What changed on the homepage

The same six changes are in all three of the new versions.

Featured In — the press logos are links now.
The client banner — larger: figures at 96px, logos at 60px.
The process section — replaced by the two-stage path, sitting below the three lenses.
Nothing pins — the page just scrolls, which is the treatment that was approved.
The hero — holds the founders’ film.
Colour — the palette is no longer limited to one background colour.
Two things are still open. The seven Featured In logos are links but have no URLs yet. And the founders’ film has not been shot — the hero currently shows the two studio portraits behind a play button, marked “to be shot”. That placeholder should not go live as it is.
