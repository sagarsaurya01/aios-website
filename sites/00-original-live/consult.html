What we look for

Three lenses, one question: where is the money?

Leakage

Money the business already earns but does not keep. Pricing that drifted, work billed short, rework nobody logs, discounts given quietly, stock that ages in a corner.

Concentration

Risk resting on one customer, one supplier, one machine, or one long-serving person who carries the process in their head and has never written it down.

Gap

Work the business could win and does not. Enquiries that go cold, capacity idle on a Tuesday, decisions taken three weeks after they mattered.

Rupees

Every finding carries a number and the working behind it.

Evidence

Drawn from your own documents and floor, not sector averages.

Built

We stay through the build and measure the result sixty days after it runs.

The path

A short free call to check fit, then two paid stages. Each ends with something written that belongs to you, and each ends with you deciding whether the next one is worth it.

Discovery

An owner interview, an Indian-sourced sector study, documents, conversations and a floor walk, a mid-point mirror where you correct us, then a final report with findings sized in rupees.

Implementation

AI built into how the business actually runs — staged, agreed against a written baseline, and measured over the sixty days after it goes live.
